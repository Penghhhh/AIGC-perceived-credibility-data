is a paired cluster bootstrap over the identical resamples.

exact within-level representation of the SEM and, unlike lavaan's
two-level ML engine, it stays numerically valid once post dummies are
added (lavaan returns wrong coefficients for that augmented model).

======================================================================
  NOTES AND REPORTING CHECKLIST
======================================================================
1. Method: two-level dual-mediation SEM (lavaan 0.7.2, MLR, BFGS);
   ALL inference is a participant-level nonparametric cluster bootstrap
   (B = 5000, seed 20240810). No Wald/z test and no delta-method SE is
   used for any structural result.
2. Resampling unit = participant (the independent sampling unit); every
   resampled participant carries all four of her/his observations, so the
   within-person design and the crossed post assignment are preserved.
3. Both percentile and BCa intervals are reported and their agreement is
   checked explicitly at the end of each table block. Report the percentile
   interval as primary (pre-specified) and disclose any disagreement.
4. Resample indices are drawn once on the master process before the
   parallel loop, so results are exactly reproducible and independent of
   the number of workers (seed printed above).
5. Point estimates come from the MLR SEM; the bootstrap only provides the
   empirical sampling distribution. Within-level SEM coefficients equal
   pooled-within OLS (verified above), which is the estimator used inside
   the bootstrap and for the with-post robustness check.
6. Multiplicity: bootstrap CIs are per-effect and unadjusted. The two
   hypothesis-bearing contrasts are 'disclosure vs. detection on perceived
   AI authorship' (H2b) and 'detection vs. disclosure on psychological
   discomfort' (H1b); if the journal requires a multiplicity adjustment,
   apply it to those two p(boot) values only. The remaining contrasts and
   all indirect/total effects are descriptive and are not adjusted.
7. Post (stimulus) was treated as a fixed blocking factor; only four
   posts exist, so a post random effect is not estimable with confidence.
8. Effect sizes to report for the mediation results are the completely
   standardized effects (beta) with their bootstrap CIs; the total-effect
   and indirect-effect betas are printed above. For indirect/total effects
   beta = b x SD(focal predictor) / SD(perceived credibility) (SD ratio
   taken from the matching direct path), because lavaan's std.all
   standardises the interaction column on its own SD (0.433) whereas the
   two dummies have SD = 0.5; adding those coefficients would mix two
   denominators and could flip the sign (see the Section 6 check).
9. The within-level residual covariance auth ~~ disc is printed with its
   robust (MLR) SE, z and p in Section 7, so that the manuscript value
   0.28 (SE = 0.09, p = .001) is traceable to this output file.
