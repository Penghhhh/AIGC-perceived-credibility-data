--- Design & Coding ---
Observations: 1860
Participants (level-2 clusters): 93
Reviews/Stimuli (crossed factor): 20
Trials per participant: all 20
Complete crossing: every participant rated every review (unique participant-review pairs = 1860)
Detection label: binary (0/1), balanced within participant (10 labeled, 10 unlabeled per participant)
  NOTE: label assignment was randomized per participant, so label VARIES WITHIN
  review across participants (verified: all 20 reviews show both labels).
Confidence in AI detection: participant-level, constant within participant (16 unique values, range 1.4-5.4),
  measured after multiple label exposures -> conditional-heterogeneity interpretation
functional_value / experiential_value / emotional_valence: content ratings measured
  AFTER label presentation (post-treatment); used only in sensitivity analysis
perceived_ai_authorship: mediator; perceived_credibility: outcome (1-7 scales)

Centering: detection_label_c = detection_label - participant mean
          (effect-coded within participant, {-0.5, +0.5});
          functional_value_c/experiential_value_c/emotional_valence_c =
          within-participant deviations (sensitivity only);
          detection_confidence_c = grand-mean centered.

  11. INTERPRETATION NOTES (for the paper)
============================================================

1. The detection-label effect on perceived AI authorship is positive and
   moderated by confidence; because confidence was measured after label
   exposure (and is participant-level), this moderation is reported as
   CONDITIONAL HETEROGENEITY of the treatment effect, not as evidence
   for a causal moderator.
2. The indirect effect label -> AI authorship -> credibility is negative
   and significant at mean and +1 SD confidence; not significant at -1 SD;
   index of moderated mediation excludes zero.
3. The direct effect is not significant -> pattern consistent with
   (full) mediation of the negative total effect.
4. Review-level variance components are large (see Section 2); all
   estimates are robust to the review fixed-effects specification
   (Section 5) and to correlated random slopes (Section 4).
5. Moderation of the AI authorship -> credibility path by functional value /
   experiential value / emotional valence (post-treatment ratings)
   is reported only in the sensitivity analysis (Section 6).
6. Label was assigned randomly per participant per review (label varies
   within review across participants), so the within-participant (group-mean
   centered) coding and the two-way cluster bootstrap are appropriate.
